Snapvero

Privacy Policy · Effective September 16, 2026

Snapvero builds before-and-after photo reports for renovation and repair work. It is an offline app: there is no account, no sign-in and no server of ours. Your projects, photos, notes, business details, PDF and Word files and portable archives stay on your device, and we have no access to them.

Who we are

The app is developed and operated by the independent developer published on the App Store and Google Play as the owner of Snapvero, acting as the data controller for the purposes of data protection law. Contact for privacy requests: 49serggor@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in the app: projects and their sections, items and before/after photo pairs, the markup you draw on photos, text blocks, captions and notes, the performer and company details you enter for the report header, your logo, generated PDF and DOCX files, and .ppair archives. None of it is uploaded anywhere by the app. When you share a report, your own system share sheet sends the file to the recipient you pick; from that moment the file is governed by whatever service you chose.

The camera is used only to take photos into the current project. The system photo picker returns the photos you select; the app copies them into its own storage and does not read the rest of your library. There is no location tag added by the app, no address book access and no microphone use.

What the app sends: diagnostics

The only data that leaves your device is pseudonymous diagnostics through Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC). This is why the Android build asks for the INTERNET permission; the app itself works fully offline.

Usage events are a fixed, closed list — no free text is ever sent:

EventMeaning
screen_viewA screen was opened, by its fixed internal name
project_createdA project was created
pdf_generatedA PDF was saved
archive_exportedAn archive file was created
archive_importedAn archive file was imported

Crash reports contain the stack trace, the screen the app was on and an anonymous code for a failed save. Project names, photos, notes, business details, file paths and identifiers are never added to these reports.

Alongside the events, the Firebase SDKs attach technical identifiers and device information: an app-instance ID, device model, OS and app version, and an approximate location (country or region) derived from your IP address by Google. That makes the data pseudonymous rather than fully anonymous. It is used for one purpose — analytics: knowing which features are used and which builds crash.

No advertising identifier is collected: the Android advertising ID permissions are removed from the app, iOS IDFV collection and ad personalisation signals are turned off, and no advertising SDK is present. We do not track you across apps or websites and there is no advertising in the app.

There is currently no in-app switch for diagnostics. If you do not want the app to send them, you can block its network access with your operating system's own controls, or uninstall the app. If a switch is added in a later version, this page will say so.

Service providers

We do not sell personal data, do not share it for advertising and have no other recipients than the two diagnostics services below.

  • Firebase Analytics (Google LLC) — the usage events listed above, app-instance ID, device and app information, IP-derived approximate location.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC) — crash logs and stack traces, device model, OS version, app version.

Google processes this data under its own privacy terms; data may be processed on servers outside your country, including in the United States. See Firebase privacy information.

Retention and deletion

Because we hold no account, there is nothing on our side to delete. Data on your device is removed when you delete a project, clear temporary files in Settings, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the app's whole storage, including projects and photos — export an archive first if you want to keep them.

The app deliberately keeps its data out of system backups: Android backup is disabled for the app, and the iOS data directory is excluded from iCloud and computer backups. Files you saved or shared yourself (a PDF in Files, a photo you re-saved) follow the rules of wherever you put them.

Diagnostics data is retained on Google's servers for limited periods: crash reports for around 90 days, analytics data for the period configured in Firebase (at most 14 months).

Your rights

You can export any project to a portable archive, correct or delete it in the app, and remove all app data by uninstalling. For questions or requests about your data, including access, correction, deletion or objection under the GDPR and comparable laws, write to 49serggor@gmail.com — we respond within 30 days. To identify the diagnostics records that concern you we would need your app-instance ID, which the app does not display; in practice the effective remedy is the one above — block network access or uninstall.

Children

Snapvero is a work tool for professionals and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new effective date. For material changes we will announce the update on this site before it takes effect.
