Snapvero

Before-and-after photo reports for renovation and repair work.

Snapvero turns the photos you already take on site into a report a client can read: the defect on the left, the finished work on the right, in the order you decide. It runs on iPhone, iPad and Android, works without an account or internet connection, and keeps every project on the device.

What it does

  • Projects with sections, items, and independent before/after photo groups.
  • Photos from the camera in a continuous series, or from the library — with an optional pass that assigns each one to “before” or “after” before it enters the item.
  • A full-screen editor: arrows, frames, opaque masks, ovals, ticks, crosses, polygons, text and hand-placed dimensions, with undo and redo.
  • Real PDF reports: ready-made layouts or a custom 1–4 × 1–4 grid, portrait or landscape, A4 or Letter, cover page, company details and logo.
  • Editable DOCX for Word, and selective export of single items or whole sections.
  • Portable .ppair archives with originals and markup, for moving a project to another device.
  • Nine interface languages, and a separately chosen report language.

What it does not do

No account, no server, no subscription, no advertising. Nothing about your projects is uploaded. The only data leaving the device is pseudonymous crash and usage diagnostics, described in the Privacy Policy.
