Snapvero

Before-and-after photo reports for renovation and repair work

Snapvero turns the photographs taken during a job into a document the client can read: the defect before the work and the finished result after it, presented side by side in the order you choose. The application runs on iPhone, iPad and Android, requires no account or internet connection, and keeps every project on the device.

Features

  • Projects organised into sections and items, each with independent before and after photograph groups.
  • Photographs taken in a continuous series with the camera or selected from the library, with an optional step that assigns each one to the before or after side.
  • A full-screen editor providing arrows, frames, opaque masks, geometric shapes, text and measurement annotations, with undo and redo.
  • PDF reports with predefined layouts or a custom grid, portrait or landscape orientation, A4 or Letter paper, a cover page, business details and a logo.
  • Editable Word documents, and selective export of individual items or whole sections.
  • Portable archives that carry the original photographs and annotations to another device.
  • Nine interface languages, with the report language selected separately.

Privacy

Snapvero has no user accounts, no cloud storage, no subscription and no advertising. Project content is never uploaded. The only information leaving the device is pseudonymous diagnostic data used to monitor stability, described in the Privacy Policy.
