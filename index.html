Snapvero

Before-and-after photo reports for renovation and repair work

Snapvero turns the photographs taken during a job into a document the client can read: the defect before the work and the finished result after it, presented side by side in the order you choose. The application runs on iPhone, iPad and Android, requires no account or internet connection, and keeps every project on the device.

What it does

  • Keeps the work of a site in one project, divided into the parts a client reads as separate items, each with its own before and after photographs.
  • Takes photographs with the camera or accepts them from the library, and sorts them onto the before or the after side.
  • Marks up a photograph full screen — pointing at what matters, covering what should not be seen, and writing on the image itself.
  • Produces a report as a document ready to send, with your business details and logo, in a layout chosen at the time of export.
  • Carries a whole project to another device as a portable archive, with the photographs and the annotations kept editable.
  • Speaks several languages, with the language of the report chosen separately from the language of the interface.

Privacy

Snapvero has no user accounts, no cloud storage, no subscription and no advertising. Project content is never uploaded. The only information leaving the device is pseudonymous diagnostic data used to monitor stability, described in the Privacy Policy.
