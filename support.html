What to include in a bug report

  • Device and OS version, for example “iPhone 14, iOS 18.5” or “Samsung S23, Android 15”.
  • App version, shown in Settings.
  • What you did, what you expected, what happened instead.
  • Whether it happens every time or once.
  • A screenshot, if the problem is visible on screen. Blur anything confidential — we do not need your client's data.

Questions we get

Where are my projects stored?

On the device only. There is no account and no cloud: nothing is uploaded, and nothing syncs between your phone and tablet. To move a project to another device, export it as a .ppair archive and import that file on the other device.

I deleted the app — can I get my projects back?

No. Uninstalling removes the app's storage, and the app is deliberately excluded from system backups so that client photos do not end up in a cloud backup. Export an archive before uninstalling, before switching phones, and after any project you would not want to redo.

The report should look different

Layout is chosen at export time: three ready-made layouts or a custom grid from 1×1 to 4×4, portrait or landscape, A4 or Letter, two quality profiles, an optional cover, and a choice of which business details are printed. The report language is picked separately from the interface language — a Russian-speaking user can send a German report.

Can I edit the report after export?

Export to DOCX and open it in Word or any compatible editor. Headings, paragraphs, sections and captions stay editable; photos are embedded with the markup already flattened into the image.

Do photos under a mask stay hidden?

In exported PDF and DOCX files, yes — masks are flattened into the pixels. A .ppair archive is different: it carries the original photos and the markup separately so the project stays editable, and the app warns you about this before the export. Send archives only to people who may see the originals.

Something failed to save

The app tells you when a write fails and keeps the change so you can retry. If it keeps failing, check free space on the device first, then write to us with the steps.

Privacy

The app sends no project data anywhere; only pseudonymous crash and usage diagnostics leave the device. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
