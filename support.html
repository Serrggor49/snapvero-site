Reporting a problem

To resolve a problem quickly, please include:

  • the device and operating system version, for example iPhone 14 with iOS 18.5, or Samsung Galaxy S23 with Android 15;
  • the application version, shown on the Settings screen;
  • the steps you took, the result you expected and the result you observed;
  • whether the problem occurs every time or occurred once;
  • a screenshot, where the problem is visible on screen.

Please obscure any confidential information before sending a screenshot; details of your clients and sites are not required in order to investigate a fault.

Frequently asked questions

Where are projects stored?

Entirely on the device. Snapvero has no user account and no cloud storage, so projects are not uploaded and do not synchronise between devices.

How do I move a project to another device?

Export the project as a portable archive and import that file on the other device. The archive contains the original photographs together with the annotations, so work can continue without loss of quality or editability.

Can projects be recovered after the application is removed?

No. Removing the application deletes its stored data, and the application is deliberately excluded from system backups so that photographs of client sites are not copied into cloud backups. We recommend exporting an archive of any project you would not want to reconstruct, and before changing devices.

How is the appearance of a report controlled?

The layout is selected at the time of export: three predefined layouts or a custom grid of up to four columns and four rows, portrait or landscape orientation, A4 or Letter paper, two quality profiles, an optional cover page, and a choice of which business details appear in the report. The pages can be previewed before the file is saved or sent.

Can a report be edited after it is exported?

Yes. Export the report in Word format and open it in Word or a compatible editor: headings, captions and paragraphs remain editable text. Photographs are embedded with their annotations already merged into the image.

Do masked areas remain hidden?

In exported PDF and Word files the mask is merged into the image, so the area beneath it is not present in the file. A portable archive is different by design: it preserves the original photographs and the annotations separately so that the project remains editable. The application warns you of this before the archive is created. Archives should therefore be sent only to recipients who may see the original photographs.

The report is produced in the wrong language

The language of the report is chosen on the export screen and is independent of the interface language, so a report may be produced in a language other than the one you work in. The interface language follows the system setting and can be changed in Settings.

A change could not be saved

The application reports a failed save and retains the change so that the operation can be repeated. If saving fails repeatedly, check the free storage space on the device and then contact us with the steps that reproduce the problem.

Privacy

Content created in the application is not transmitted anywhere. The only information leaving the device is pseudonymous diagnostic data, described in full in the Privacy Policy.
